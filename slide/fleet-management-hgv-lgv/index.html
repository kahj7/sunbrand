Fleet Management

From qualitative research to new experiences

How can we extend HERE’s Logistics current selective API and rest extension offers along the customer end-to-end experience from fulfilment centres to final delivery, through a heavy or light carriers.

Understand the end-to-end fleet management use case to identify new customer-centric touchpoints for HERE.

Challenge

HERE was providing selective API and rest extensions for the Logistic market. The challenge was to build on existing business and extend the current offering without cannibalising HERE’s revenue (of existing contracts).  Secondly the growing on-demand market, which internally was addressed as a consumer offer focused on the e-hailing use case only, asked for a stronger interaction between logistic and consumer business groups to ensure transparency, align objectives, and efficiency.

Approach

A qualitative research on fleet management was carried out in the UK and US on light and heavy goods carriers to understand the end-to-end journey of drivers and managers. This formed the base of several cross-functional workshops. Gaps and frustrations for the actors along those use-cases were identified and translated into new solutions leveraging HERE expertise.

Outcome

The solutions defined influenced the short and long-term roadmap and portfolio planning in the areas of data collection and application & feature development. Selected projects were moved into an experimental prototyping process to test the hypotheses about feasibility, customer & market fit further before phasing back to development.

Role

I identified the initiative in collaboration with the Logistics Executive Team and supervised the innovation management, including qualitative research, problem understanding, solution ideation, prototyping and validation, feasibility and viability assessment.
