Navigation for Samsung Gear S

From customer proposal to product release

HERE partnered with Samsung to bring its maps and location platform services to the newly-announced Samsung Gear S and subsequently to all Samsung’s Tizen- powered smart devices.

This partnership not only brought HERE a multi million dollar deal, but also broadened its reach to users and businesses across screens and operating systems.

Challenge

Understand the customers proposition and capabilities to showcase experiences valuable for both, the client and HERE in order to win partnership contract.
Develop and launch an application for a hardware which itself is being built at the same time. To get the development processes for both the application and the device between HERE and Samsung in sync was particularly challenging.

Approach

Working hand in hand with Samsung development team with daily SW exchange to translate HERE’s maps and location offers into a glance-able experience for Samsung’s Gear S.
This meant handling troubleshooting with device connectivity, accuracy of positioning.

Outcome

HERE for Gear was the first application which offered turn-by-turn walk navigation and public transit routing on a wearable device.
The product was released on the IFA 2014, with a concept to release development time of 3 month.

Role

I lead all stages of product design including ideation, identification, evaluation, pitch case, UI/UX, IA, product strategy, visual, user research, and launch.
