2013 • Product Definition • Design Vision & strategy

Co-Driver

Your personal driving assistant – A new product category

The Co-driver is a personal driving assistance that provides timely information to the driver on daily paths or unknown routes by bringing together driver behaviour, realtime context and map data.

A new in-car experience that disrupts the connected driving market and leads towards autonomous driving.

Challenge

2013 the traditional PND market was in steep decline. Devices in the market focused on map and navigation from A to B only, failed to provide meaningful contextual information and offered no media or telematics integration.
On the OEMs side innovation for in-build navigation systems was challenged by long and restricted development cycles.

Approach

Define a differentiating & contemporary in-car experience that is truly desirable for the user, enabled by leveraging HERE’s current core map assets, and also supports HERE’s strategy of becoming a cloud company that collects, interprets and combines different data to provide meaningful predictions.

Outcome

A new product offering that combines detailed map data (e.g. speed limit), real time data (e.g. icy road) and driver preferences and driving style (e.g. how fast do I prefer to take a curve)
Concept & prototype were successfully pitched to C- level Leadership for product development funding and became 1 of 4 company innovation programs for strategic growth within HERE.

Role

I led the design vision, product definition, product design and alignment of strategic goals of the project with the strategy of the company through communications with C-level management.

 

Collaboration

C-Level engagement, Mobility & Automotive Leadership, Data Science, Product marketing

Experience Prototype Road Warning – Keeps you and your family safe on the road. Nothing is more important than you arriving at your destination safe and sound. Co-Driver keeps you informed about the road ahead. No matter what’s coming – an oil spill, hairpin turn or sudden bad weather – Co-Driver will let you know, so you can make better decisions, avoid traffic and stay safe.

Personalised for you and your journey – No two drives or drivers are the same. So why should you get the same as everyone else? Co-Driver is a truly personal product. It learns the way you drive and adapts to your driving style and habits. Don’t like to drive through tunnels? Prefer not to take toll roads? Whatever your personal style, Co-Driver gives you what’s right for you in the right moment.
